# Skins

Udeos has its own skin system, so you can wear a custom skin without a Minecraft
account. Every profile has its own library.

![The skin library](../assets/screenshots/skins.png)

## The Skins page

- Wearing: the skin you have on, on a 3D model you can turn by dragging.
- Drop a skin you downloaded: drop a `.png` anywhere on the window, or press
  Browse files. You name it, the launcher guesses the model (Classic or Slim)
  and you can wear it right away.
- Library: click a card to wear that skin. Edit and Delete appear when you hover
  over a card. Steve is always there as the default.
- New skin opens the editor on a blank Steve (or Alex for a Slim skin).

The skin you wear is used by every instance of your profile from the next Play,
and its face becomes your avatar in the launcher.

## Classic and Slim

Skins come in two shapes: Classic arms are 4 pixels wide (Steve), Slim arms are
3 (Alex). The same picture looks wrong on the other shape, so each skin
remembers its model. If the arms look cut or show a black stripe, switch the
model.

Old 64×32 skins are converted to the modern 64×64 format automatically.

## The editor

![The skin editor](../assets/screenshots/skin-editor.png)

You paint straight on the 3D model. The flat skin is in the side panel.

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| Paint | Click or drag on the model |
| Turn the model | Right-drag |
| Move the view | Shift-drag or middle-drag |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel (toward the pointer), or + and − |
| Reset the view | 0 |
| Pencil, eraser, fill, color picker | B, E, G, I |
| Undo, redo | Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y |

The Second layer switch paints the outer layer (hat, jacket, sleeves,
trousers). The Parts figure hides the head, arms or legs so you can reach what
is behind them. The palette has Minecraft's dye colours, skin tones and your
recent colours. When zoomed in, a pixel grid appears.

If you leave the editor without saving, your work is kept as a draft and
offered again next time.

## Where your skin shows

| Where | Shows? |
|-------|--------|
| Singleplayer | Yes, on every version |
| Your own servers set to "Udeos players" | Yes, and every Udeos player there sees it |
| Your own servers set to "Anyone" | No |
| Public servers | Usually not, they use their own skin system |

A friend's own custom skin does not show on your server, because each launcher
only knows its own profiles' skins.
