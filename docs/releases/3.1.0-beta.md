Udeos Launcher is a small program to install and play Minecraft on Windows,
macOS and Linux. **You do not need a Microsoft account.** Pick a name, pick a
version, press Play.

If something breaks, please
[open an issue](https://github.com/Juan17la/Udeos_Launcher/issues) and say
what you did.

## What is new

- **Your last game has its own panel.** The instance you played last now sits
  in a tall panel on the right of your library: when you played, for how long,
  what is in it, a big Play button, Manage and Folder.
- **Fold the sidebar.** The bar on the left can shrink to just icons, and the
  launcher remembers your choice.
- **See which servers are on.** Servers that are running, starting or
  stopping are highlighted, with a green dot when they are online.

## Changes

- The dark theme is a softer night blue instead of almost black.
- More Minecraft items float faintly in the background of every page.
- Your instances show three to a row on a full screen, and stay two to a row
  until the window gets really small.
- Less empty space around your instances and the last played panel.

## Fixes

- Switching profiles no longer flashes the new name over the old instances.
- Opening a mod's details no longer blinks "Loading", and the Install button
  no longer flashes blue before saying Installed.

## Good to know

- **Online servers.** Servers that check for a Microsoft account will not let
  you in. Single player works, and so do servers set to "offline mode".
- **The installers are not signed yet.** Windows and macOS show a warning the
  first time you open the launcher. The table below says how to get past it.
