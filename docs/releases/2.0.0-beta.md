Udeos Launcher is a small program to install and play Minecraft on Windows,
macOS and Linux. **You do not need a Microsoft account.** Pick a name, pick a
version, press Play.

This is a new major version. If something breaks, please
[open an issue](https://github.com/Juan17la/Udeos_Launcher/issues) and say
what you did.

## What is new

- **A calmer first start.** The welcome screen asks one thing at a time:
  your name, then the language, then the look. Use Next and Back to move
  around.
- **Easier to choose a look.** Dark, Light and Custom are listed with a
  short explanation. With Custom you pick light or dark and your own colour.

## Good to know

- **Online servers.** Servers that check for a Microsoft account will not let
  you in. Single player works, and so do servers set to "offline mode".
