Udeos Launcher is a small program to install and play Minecraft on Windows,
macOS and Linux. **You do not need a Microsoft account.** Pick a name, pick a
version, press Play.

This is a small update with two fixes. If something breaks, please
[open an issue](https://github.com/Juan17la/Udeos_Launcher/issues) and say
what you did.

## What is new

- **Dropping files works on Windows.** Dragging a mod, a world, a datapack or
  a skin onto the launcher did nothing on Windows. It works now.
- **Steadier layout in small windows.** The tabs of an instance or a server
  stay on one row, the side panels and cards keep their size, and only the
  lists of cards scroll. The skin editor fits the window too.

## Good to know

- **Online servers.** Servers that check for a Microsoft account will not let
  you in. Single player works, and so do servers set to "offline mode".
