Udeos Launcher is a small program to install and play Minecraft on Windows,
macOS and Linux. **You do not need a Microsoft account.** Pick a name, pick a
version, press Play.

This is the third beta. It works, but it is still young: if something
breaks, please
[open an issue](https://github.com/Juan17la/Udeos_Launcher/issues) and say
what you did.

## What's new

**Ask AI for addons.** On the Addons page, press **Ask AI** and write what
you want: "performance mods for fabric 1.20.1", "a skyblock modpack",
"medieval textures". The AI searches Modrinth and shows you its best picks
right in the chat, each with a short reason why it fits and its own **Add**
and **Details** buttons. Ask again to narrow it down ("only forge", "newer
ones"), or press **See all** to get the full list on the page. You can also
ask from an instance's Mods, Resource Packs and Shaders tabs, and the picks
will fit that instance.

**Free, nothing to set up.** Ask AI works right away. If you have your own
API key from Groq, Claude, OpenAI, Gemini or Grok, you can use it instead
(the list shows which models come with a free plan). Your key stays on your
computer.

**Update from inside the launcher.** When a new version is out, the
launcher tells you on start. Press **Update** and it downloads and installs
the right file for your system. **Later** asks again next time.

**Slow first starts say so.** The first time you play a Minecraft version,
the launcher downloads the whole game. The loading window now says so with
a gold **First install** note, so you know it is working and not frozen.
The same goes for a server's first start.

**Smoother look.** Search results and your instances slide in when they
appear, and searches show placeholder cards while they load. Text boxes
have a clear border that lights up when you click into them. Adding a mod
shows a small, quiet loader instead of a big loading box.

## Still to come

- **Optimization.** Not done yet: some screens can feel slow, and the
  launcher can use more memory and CPU than it should. This is the next
  thing we are working on.

## Good to know

- **Online servers.** Servers that check for a Microsoft account will not let
  you in. Single player works, and so do servers set to "offline mode".
- **AI answers can be wrong.** The AI only picks from what Modrinth really
  has, and **Add** still checks that everything fits your instance, but its
  picks and reasons are suggestions. If it is busy, try again in a minute or
  use your own key.
- **The installers are not signed yet.** Windows and macOS show a warning the
