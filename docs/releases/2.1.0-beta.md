Udeos Launcher is a small program to install and play Minecraft on Windows,
macOS and Linux. **You do not need a Microsoft account.** Pick a name, pick a
version, press Play.

If something breaks, please
[open an issue](https://github.com/Juan17la/Udeos_Launcher/issues) and say
what you did.

## What is new

- **Make your skin on the 3D player.** The skin editor now shows a big player
  you paint on directly. Scroll to zoom in close, right-drag to turn it, and
  hold Shift while dragging to move it. Hide the head, arms or legs to paint
  the parts behind them. There is a colour palette with Minecraft's colours
  and skin tones, and a pixel grid when you zoom in.
- **Delete is easier to find.** Instances and servers now have a Delete
  button right next to Edit. It still asks before deleting anything.

## Fixed

- **Ask AI no longer shows a blank window.** Sometimes the first question
  left the launcher empty until you restarted it. The answer now shows up as
  it should.

## Good to know

- **Online servers.** Servers that check for a Microsoft account will not let
  you in. Single player works, and so do servers set to "offline mode".
